I have a [Sphinx-generated site](http://lassoguide.com/) ([source](https://github.com/LassoSoft/LassoGuide/tree/0cc1f2a9b214b07d43e376d6a4d74fca0cf5a5e1)) in which the "Site" dropdown is generated in the template by the [toctree()](http://www.sphinx-doc.org/en/stable/templating.html#toctree) callable [here](https://github.com/LassoSoft/LassoGuide/blob/master/source/_themes/amphibious/globaltoc.html). It's called with `maxdepth=2` to make it list all the parts in the entire book, and the other chapters in the same part as the current chapter.

However, Sphinx frequently and inconsistently does not recognize where the current chapter is in the toctree, and as a result the "Site" dropdown only shows the book's parts, with no other chapters. For example, the last time it was built, this page's dropdown [correctly shows the part's other chapters](http://lassoguide.com/old/operations/os-process.html), but this page's dropdown is [missing the list of other chapters](http://lassoguide.com/old/api/lcapi-overview.html). 

What's strange is how inconsistent the results are. For example, if I make a fresh HTML build, anywhere from 15 to 55 of the 77 total pages will have their dropdowns built correctly (as indicated by the number of resulting HTML pages containing the text "toctree-l1 current"). If I `touch` one of the source .rst files, it may or may not come out fixed on the next build, and if I touch them all with `find . -name *.rst -exec touch {} +`, I'll get a different result again. The only pages that are consistently built correctly are the index pages for each part ([example](http://lassoguide.com/api/index_api.html)). So far, the only fix is to call `toctree` with `collapse=False` and hide the chapters in the other parts with CSS.
