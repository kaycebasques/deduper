There is one easy reason why it is the way it is:  Simplicity.  All builders produce their output to `build/`, and so does the _gettext_ builder.

I, for one, would disagree with your tree structure and expect it the other way around:  `.pot` files are build products so they land in `build/`.  (They can be deleted without any loss.)  On the other hand, `.mo` files are _sources_(!) so they should go into `source/`, no?  (If you lose them, your translations are gone.)
