When `toctree_only` is disabled, the LaTeX builder places the `startdoc` contents in a chapter without a title. The title of this chapter is "Contents", as displayed in the page footer.

I'm thinking this could be improved upon, but I'm not sure how. Perhaps the startdoc contents could be rendered as a foreword or preface, or perhaps as a chapter of which the title can be configured (default to "Introduction")? I guess this could depend on the document template used (article or book).
