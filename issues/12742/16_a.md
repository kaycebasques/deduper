> If I had to guess at the reason for three space indentation, it is simply that explicit markup in reST uses `\.\. +\w\s*::`, and hence the minimum amount of leading indentation is three (`.. `). 

Actually, the minimal indentation is ONE. 
Example:
~~~
.. topic:: topic title

 Topic text.

*
 bullet list
 
.. code:: python
 :name: world
 
 print("hello world")
~~~
This may help when browsing rST sources on a smartphone :)
