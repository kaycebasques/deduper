> so why does it have no spaces at the beginning?

The [general rules of indentation always hold][1]; that's to say the parser will count the number of spaces (indentation level) of the first non-blank line and expect any subsequent lines within a given block to be consistent relative to that level.

> so why does it have no spaces at the beginning?

Because the parser strips indentation when processing a directive.
