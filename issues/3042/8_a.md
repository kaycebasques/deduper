With #13705 merged, `tabulary` is used more often: formerly cells containing lists in particular would cause Sphinx to drop `tabulary`.  It is thus a possibility that this issue will be encountered more often.  I have tried to re-improve our docs at #13711.  

The problem as I understand it (now that I again thought about it), is really ultimately caused by a defect of `tabulary`'s algorithm.  I have not yet looked into its internals, because doing modifications there is not good for future LaTeX maintenance of Sphinx.
