In my brief investigation, this might prove a Docutils issue. Indeed with:

```rst
.. py:function:: foo()

   :param foo: A table.

               ======= ===
               a "foo" bar
               ======= ===
```

the smart quote is correct.

The issue is that in your original example Docutils handles the string ``."foo"``  where the dot comes from ``table.`` and is treated as "context".

In my example here, Docutils handles the string ``a "foo"`` and identifies ``"foo"`` as a quoted word.
