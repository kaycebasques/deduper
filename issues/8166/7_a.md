According to the Docutils document model, a [math_block element](https://docutils.sourceforge.io/docs/ref/doctree.html#math-block) is not allowed inside a paragraph.

You may consider defining and styling a special role:
~~~~
.. role:: displaymath(math)

paragraph starts
:displaymath:`\displaystyle \int f(x) = \sin^2(x)`
continues  
~~~~

This could result in the desired "semantic" HTML, however, styling an inline-element nested in `<p>` into a centred equation block is tricky.
OTOH, styling the HTML output of  the "compound" directive to get less vertical spacing is easy.

The spurious "\noindent" in LaTeX output is specific to the Sphinx LaTeX writer and not present in up-to-date Docutils. The Docutils LaTeX writer also takes the pragmatic decision not to write blank lines before/after a math-block. In the rare case that a formula starts/ends a paragraph, an empty comment may serve as delimiter in the rst source.
