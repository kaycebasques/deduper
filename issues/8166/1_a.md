**Is your feature request related to a problem? Please describe.**

I would like to include an equation inside a paragraph. This is [customary](https://tex.stackexchange.com/a/566/51635) in math-y texts, where breaking a paragraph on equation is often incorrect since an equation may be a part of a sentence. Right now display math can only be created using a directive, which necessarily breaks the paragraph.

**Describe the solution you'd like**

I propose to implement a new role, e.g. ``:math:display:`content` ``, or anything alike, which creates a `math_block` nodes in the doctree.
