Unfortunately I am not having luck using `autodoc_use_legacy_class_based` to get a [custom `AttributeDocumenter`](https://github.com/spacetelescope/stpipe/blob/9c349cbfdcdb7d744656d55c2d0bbd0f62f8e601/docs/source/conf.py#L16) to work with sphinx 9.

It looks like the new autodoc code registers directives on the `config-inited` event:
https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/blob/cc7c6f435ad37bb12264f8118c8461b230e6830c/sphinx/ext/autodoc/__init__.py#L207
which changes the order that directives are registered compared to sphinx 8. With sphinx 8 the extensions (autodoc) were registered before `setup` was called. This allowed the custom `AttributeDocumenter` to overwrite the autodoc attribute documenter. With sphinx 9 the order is reversed and autodoc appears to now be overwriting the custom one.
