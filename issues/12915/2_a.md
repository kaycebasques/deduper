Do you expect to ignore additional URI schemes other than `man:` when using this?  The reason I ask is that it would be fairly straightforward to add that scheme to the ignore-list [alongside `mailto:` and `tel:`](https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/blob/043750e406d8fe03953f5b565b36e05aef6866ec/sphinx/builders/linkcheck.py#L380-L381) in the codebase.

I'll also note that `man:` doesn't appear to be a registered (or provisional) [IANA URI scheme](https://www.iana.org/assignments/uri-schemes/uri-schemes.xhtml) at the moment - but if it's supported by Firefox (and/or other browsers?) perhaps it's worth considering anyway.
