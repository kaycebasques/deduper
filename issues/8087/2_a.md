I tried with adding a raw latex directive:
```rest
.. raw:: latex

   \setcounter{secnumdepth}{-1}

==========
Unnumbered
==========

Bravo
=====

Charlie
-------
```

I also added to preamble ``\setcounter{tocdepth}{3}``.  This gives this TOC in PDF:
