### Cross-referencing with partial title.

It's possible to shorten a cross reference by prepending `~`. E.g. ``:py:meth:`~Queue.Queue.get` will only display "get".

I would like to have a way of partial shortening, i.e. display "Queue.get" in the above example.

Of course, one can write an explicit title ``:py:meth:`Queue.get <Queue.Queue.get>` ``. However that's very verbose. I propose to allow the `~` also within the pattern after a `.` to display a partial title, i.e. ``:py:meth:`Queue.~Queue.get` ``.
