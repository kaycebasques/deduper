It appears that this is due to the fact that `ifconfig` nodes are only resolved when the doctree-resolved event is emitted, but environment consistency is checked earlier than that.

Perhaps `ifconfig` nodes could be resolved earlier, directly while the source is being read?

Conversely, `only` nodes suffer from the opposite problem: they are always parsed and then later either included or not depending on the tag, so the "document isn't included in any toctree warning" doesn't appear if a document is only "included" through a deactivated `only` (similar test case as above).
