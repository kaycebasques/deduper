The obvious workaround is "make clean" but you obviously do _not_ want to do that when dealing with large and very time-consuming builds like in https://github.com/zephyrproject-rtos/zephyr/issues/99932. 

Fortunately, there is a very fast and not too inconvenient workaround: refresh the timestamp of all relevant source files. Either "save" from your editor or with something like `touch $(git diff --name-only)` or `git grep --name-only 'graphviz' '*.rst'` or `touch $(git grep --name-only 'graphviz' '**.rst)`

`rm build/images/graphviz-*` can also help.
