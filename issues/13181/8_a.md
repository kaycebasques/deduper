But that's not what I'm about :) My aim is not to speed up the construction of the final monolith, just to be able to construct a partial PDF containing only the few pages corresponding to the modified source file(s).

For example, our [LaTeX FAQ](https://faq.gutenberg-asso.fr/) is made up of almost 1400 source files (`.md`) and [the complete PDF](https://www.gutenberg-asso.fr/IMG/pdf/faqlatexgutenberg.pdf.pdf) is over 2050 pages long. When a contributor modifies something in one of the 1400 source files (`.md`), for example the one corresponding to [this short FAQ](https://faq.gutenberg-asso.fr/4_domaines_specialises/mathematiques/equations/numerotation_d_equations/changer_la_numerotation_des_equations.html), he would be interested in checking the PDF output of the modified file, which is quickly constructed because it contains only (or almost only) the one or two corresponding pages.
