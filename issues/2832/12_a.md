Just a general comment from my experience using `\rowcolors`: unfortunately, this only produces good results when printed. For on-screen display, most PDF viewers will have issues (see [SE question](https://tex.stackexchange.com/questions/490663/solution-for-disappearing-table-cell-borders-with-rowcolor-in-the-pdf-viewer-de)).

Maybe at some point it could make sense to offer a `nicematrix` backend instead-of/in-addition-to the current `tabulary` and `tabu backends in Sphinx.
