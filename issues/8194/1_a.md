**Description**

When a hyperlink phrase reference is embedded in an emphasis block, `make html` does not recognize it.

**To Reproduce**

```
$ cat << EOF > source/no-joy.rst

*New users stumbling onto this may be better served by the `PDP-11 Processor Handbook`_ 
from the University of Calgary, Department of Computer Science, which is a mere 19 years old.*

----

.. _PDP-11 Processor Handbook: http://pages.cpsc.ucalgary.ca/~dsb/PDP11/
EOF

$ make clean
$ make html
```

**Expected behavior**

> *New users stumbling onto this may be better served by the [PDP-11 Processor Handbook](http://pages.cpsc.ucalgary.ca/~dsb/PDP11/) from the University of Calgary, Department of Computer Science, which is a mere 19 years old.*

**Observed behavior**

> *New users stumbling onto this may be better served by the PDP-11 Processor Handbook from the University of Calgary, Department of Computer Science, which is a mere 19 years old.*

I was able to sort of work around it by excluding the hyperlink reference from the  emphasis:


```
$ cat << EOF > source/no-joy.rst

*New users stumbling onto this may be better served by the* `PDP-11 Processor Handbook`_ 
*from the University of Calgary, Department of Computer Science, which is a mere 19 years old.*

----

.. _PDP-11 Processor Handbook: http://pages.cpsc.ucalgary.ca/~dsb/PDP11/
EOF

$ make clean
$ make html
```

which yielded the less pretty

> *New users stumbling onto this may be better served by the* [PDP-11 Processor Handbook](http://pages.cpsc.ucalgary.ca/~dsb/PDP11/) *from the University of Calgary, Department of Computer Science, which is a mere 19 years old.*
