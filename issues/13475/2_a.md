I have been pondering makeing it possible to explicitly overwrite signatures to allow better customization. For example here
https://github.com/matplotlib/matplotlib/blob/main/doc/api/pyplot_summary.rst#basic

```
.. autosummary::
   :template: autosummary.rst

   plot(x, y, ...)
```
would make sense. The two parameters are the relevant ones, more would be clutter in the summary, less would be less informative.

The rule could be: If a signature is explicitly written out for the respective function, that takes precedence over the `:signatures:` setting. If `()` would be sufficient for you, your use case would be included. Otherwise, one could consider a special marker on the relevant entry (`MyClass -` or `MyClass :signatures: none` or similar).

Either way since signatures is more than a binary flag now and since needs could vary across entries, I believe that the way to make this customizable per entry must be realized through an annotation of the entries not though a new global parameter.
