**Is your feature request related to a problem? Please describe.**
The way the TODOs/links are listed in the list of TODOs is confusing. 

For instance, if you have a quick look at [this list](https://faq.gutenberg-asso.fr/8_contribuer/questions_a_reviser.html) and scroll down in it, the couples:

1. TODOs ("À faire" boxes),
2. links ("Lien vers la question concernée" for "Link to the relevant question"),
 
are displayed successively without any markers between them. Hence, you may not be sure whether the links refers to the previous TODO or the next TODO and you have to go at the top of the list to know it for sure.

**Describe the solution you'd like**
I suggest adding a visual marker that delimits each couple of TODO/link from the other ones.

**Describe alternatives you've considered**
The marker could be a horizontal line. Another solution could be to put each TODO/link in an item of an unordered list.
