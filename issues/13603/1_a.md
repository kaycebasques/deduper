**Is your feature request related to a problem? Please describe.**

It is common to include an email address in the author field for example see:
- https://github.com/scipopt/PySCIPOpt/blob/master/docs/conf.py#L25
- https://github.com/hypershell/hypershell/blob/master/docs/conf.py#L21

This is left as is and not transformed to @email{contact@@my.email}

**Describe the solution you'd like**

Either add an explicit email contact field or standardize having the contact email in
the author field in the conf.py file

**Describe alternatives you've considered**
Have suggested two options, standardizing the format of the email contact in the
author field seems the easiest.
