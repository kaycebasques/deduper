Our solution for (color) emoji in the PDF output was to [use LuaLaTeX](https://github.com/club-1/docs/blob/9c175ccd2dc4810d1dd4f9f0968128029bd5d3a2/conf.py#L261-L262) and to [define TwemojiMozilla as the fallback font](https://github.com/club-1/docs/blob/9c175ccd2dc4810d1dd4f9f0968128029bd5d3a2/_templates/club1.sty#L8-L18).

You can see the result there: https://club1.fr/docs/en/club1-en-latest.pdf

The idea mainly came from this article: https://www.overleaf.com/learn/latex/Articles/An_overview_of_technologies_supporting_the_use_of_colour_emoji_fonts_in_LaTeX
