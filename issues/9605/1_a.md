**Is your feature request related to a problem? Please describe.**

My jinja2 templates sometimes have spelling mistakes in them or some data provided to jinja2 has a change in format and the jinja2 template is then out of date.

In these cases, the jinja2 template engine will silently replace undefined properties with an empty string.

**Describe the solution you'd like**

There should be an option to enable errors on undefined jinja2 attributes.

**Describe alternatives you've considered**

I've tried to hack my way around this by modifying the sphinx-datatemplates plugin. 

I want error messages when expanding datatemplate jinja2 templates, but not when jinja2 is used from HTML templates.

https://github.com/sphinx-contrib/datatemplates/pull/85
