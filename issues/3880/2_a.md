IMO, the problem is the different quotes in the docstring (single quotes around `'my'`) vs. the comment (double quotes around "comment" etc.)

The smartquotes transform treats double-qutoe characters as outer quotes and single-quote characters as single quotes. Turning off the smartquotes transform would show a similar result (with straight single vs. double quotes).

`"`-characters work fine in a triple-quoted docstring.
The triple-quotes around the docstring are a Python syntax element (just like the comment character in the doc-comment), they don't make the docstring to a quotation. This means it is neither necessary nor logical to use second level quotes in a docstring.

I suggest closing this issue as "wontfix" or "invalid".
