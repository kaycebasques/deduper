> [@gmilde](https://github.com/gmilde) are [#14400](https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/pull/14400) and [#14540](https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/pull/14540) all that are needed to support docutils 0.23?

I don't know for sure. Actually, the basics should work with Docutils < 1.0 even without fixing these two issues:
#14400 is required when changing the [legacy_ids](https://docutils.sourceforge.io/docs/user/config.html#legacy-ids) setting to False, 
#14540 is required to work with Docutils 1.0.

I suggest a new CI test with Docutils 0.23.
Also, there is [a long list of changes](https://docutils.sourceforge.io/RELEASE-NOTES.html#release-0-23-2026-05-27), some backwards-incompatible, so there may be issues with non-tested features and extensions.
