We had some confusion recently about the recommendation of `:label:` rather than `:name:` for the math directives:

In [the Sphinx math directive section](https://www.sphinx-doc.org/en/master/usage/restructuredtext/directives.html#directive-math) it recommends using `label` in order to get numbered equations. 

However, the [docutils `math` directive page](https://docutils.sourceforge.io/docs/ref/rst/directives.html#math) does not list `:label:` and does list `:name:` (similar to how other directives get unique IDs).

Can Sphinx document the reason that it does not recommend using `:name:` and instead recommends `:label:` to avoid confusion to users that are referencing both sets of documentation?
