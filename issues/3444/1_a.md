The non-strict greater than and smaller than symbols, ≥ and ≤, are not defined in the TeX builder of Sphinx. I've studied the case and found that the perhaps best solution is having these definitions in the preamble:
```
\\DeclareUnicodeCharacter{2265}{\\ensuremath{\\geq}}
\\DeclareUnicodeCharacter{2264}{\\ensuremath{\\leq}}
```

I wanted to contribute this to Sphinx, but the question is where? Should this go into the `ADDITIONAL_SETTINGS` of [sphinx/writers/latex.py](https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/blob/71d0f5069f16bd1fe3f71e7c5c7484bb553507c0/sphinx/writers/latex.py) where `\\nobreakspace` is defined similarly, or should it rather go into [sphinx/util/texescape.py](https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/blob/6fa0262802a09050e09445c9fd630c69b5ad1204/sphinx/util/texescape.py) where most unicode definitions seem to be?
