Is there another trick to make `` :meth:`Child.x` `` work? Of course I could write `` :meth:`Child.x() <Parent.x>` `` but that would be unreadable and difficult to maintain.

```python
class Other:

	def x() -> None:
		pass
```
but at least it gives a warning in that case.
