Unfortunately I currently see no obvious way to modify aligned amsmath environment to produce centered equations in absence of `&` markers and normal aligned behaviour in presence of `&`.

- you could avoid using multiple equations with `math` directive,
- or use https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/issues/3785#issuecomment-303162341 with ``:nowrap:`` and `gather*`.

In both cases this means modifying your sources, which may be too much time time costly. With some script you could do that semi-automatically, but there is the problem I mentioned at top of thread that some `&` might come from `matrix`, and not be relevant to the alignment.
