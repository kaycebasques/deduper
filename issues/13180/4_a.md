The way I see it this kind of special case ordering should be implemented by the Numpydoc extension - otherwise, why use Numpydoc at all and not just use the Napoleon extension?

The Numpydoc extension isn't the *Numpy docstring format* per se. Napoleon parses the Numpy docstrings correctly but the custom ordering you're proposing: to automatically extract specific docstring sections and put them in a custom order that differs from the regular declaration order - or to enforce a specific order on authors; (in the docstring and in the rST) doesn't have any precedent in the Napoleon extension and is specific to the Numpydoc extension (and again, you fail to provide any example and so too do the linked posts). It may even be desirable to permit Napoleon users the flexibility of writing the docstrings however they wish and render them as-is (since the Numpy style guide itself starts by stating that it's a best practice recommendation and the syntax is amiss about what to do if sections are declared out of order - something that arguably needn't be enforced downstream).
