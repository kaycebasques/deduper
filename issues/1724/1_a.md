There is the convenient shorthand `:obj:`~top.module.submodule.Class`` for writing `:obj:`Class <top.module.submodule.Class>``.

I suggest to extend this syntax to multiple tilde's, to show several last component of the fully-qualified name:
- `:obj:`~~top.module.submodule.Class`` as shorthand for `:obj:`submodule.Class <topmodule.submodule.Class>`` 
- `:obj:`~~~top.module.submodule.Class`` as shorthand for `:obj:`module.submodule.Class <top.module.submodule.Class>``
- (and so on)
