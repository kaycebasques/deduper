> In the context of testing, it'd also be nice if Sphinx exposed a pytest fixture for this.

I recently discovered that https://www.sphinx-doc.org/en/master/extdev/testing.html exists and even attempted using it. One of the problems it has is that it's totally undocumented, and another is that the fixture names aren't made for use externally. A third-party project would make use of it by injecting `app` and redefining `root_dir` — both names are so generic that it's impossible to guess what these are w/o prior knowledge.
