**Is your feature request related to a problem? Please describe.**
I'm trying to create a table of contents, split into two parts, like so:
```rst
.. toctree::
   :maxdepth: 2
   :numbered:
   :caption: Part 1

   chapter-1
   chapter-2

.. toctree::
   :maxdepth: 2
   :numbered:
   :caption: Part 2

   chapter-3
   chapter-4
```

What I get is:
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/25718989/90302350-f99fcd80-dee8-11ea-951a-3cf4e77739fd.png)
What I'd like is:
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/25718989/90302362-0ae8da00-dee9-11ea-9ca8-b6a4ab4767a7.png)


**Describe the solution you'd like**
Either the ability to continue numbering from other toctrees, or (probably easier and would work just fine) the ability to set a flag e.g. 
```rst
.. toctree::
   :maxdepth: 2
   :numbered:
   :caption: Part 2
   :start-from: 3

   chapter-3
   chapter-4
```

**Describe alternatives you've considered**
moving the two toctrees into one toctree, or typing the numbering manually
