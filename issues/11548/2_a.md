When you say "signature", do you mean the "content" of your variable instead? Because there is no real concept of a "signature" for a variable (unless you are talking about "name + annotation + value").

For functions and methods, we have a way to split signatures in order for them not to exceed a certain number of characters. This was introduced in 6.2 or 7.0 (I don't remember) and you'll need to enable it with [python_maximum_signature_line_length](https://www.sphinx-doc.org/en/master/usage/configuration.html#confval-python_maximum_signature_line_length).

If you are fine with hiding the value of your list/dict, you can use [`:no-value:`](https://www.sphinx-doc.org/en/master/usage/extensions/autodoc.html#directive-autoproperty) or `#: :meta hide-value:` as a type comment.
