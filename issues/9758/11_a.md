As I said, you have to do **nothing** in your configuration. Just put the images into the same folder with language postfix. You can check the [default configuration](https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/blob/master/sphinx/config.py#L102):

```python
'figure_language_filename': ('{root}.{language}{ext}', 'env', [str]),
```

Any other settings are irrelevant. Maybe it can be important to use `figure::` in the source instead of `image::`
