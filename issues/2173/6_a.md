`Text` nodes are not post-processed for markup.  You'd need to create a `docutils.parsers.rst.RSTParser` and an empty document (`docutils.utils.new_document`), parse your source into the document, and then extract the resulting nodes.  See the [`Locale` transform](https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/blob/1.3.3/sphinx/transforms.py#L269-L271) for an example.

(`DefaultSubstitutions` won't help, since these are used as `Text` nodes as well.)
