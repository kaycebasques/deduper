From the docs:

> _"Since mathematical notation isn’t natively supported by HTML in any way..."_

That's not _totally_ true. [MathML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MathML) is a part of the HTML5 spec, though browser support is spotty to say the least. Since the alternative without installing plugins is just math not working, maybe a good default would be to follow the spec for the output format?
