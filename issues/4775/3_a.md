Try with this at top of your ``references.rst``.

```rst
.. _refs:

.. raw:: latex

   \begingroup
   \renewcommand\chapter[1]{\endgroup}

References
==========

.. [J11]
   Järvi L, Grimmond CSB & Christen A (2011) The Surface Urban Energy
   and Water Balance Scheme (SUEWS): Evaluation in Los Angeles and
   Vancouver. J. Hydrol. 411, 219-237.

```

If there had not been ``.. _refs:`` I would have used

```
.. raw:: latex

   \phantomsection
   \begingroup
   \renewcommand\chapter[1]{\endgroup}
```

Hope it helps.
