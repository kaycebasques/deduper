Interesting proposal, even if I'm not sure of the details.

I think just trying to keep the word in the translated string is hard because you don't know where the word was translated to.  Say, from the original `a b <c>` with `c` being untranslatable, you cannot easily infer which words in the translation `x y z` was `c` originally.

One thing that could work is using _gettext_'s [placeholder syntax](https://www.gnu.org/software/gettext/manual/html_node/c_002dformat.html) to insert untranslatable chunks so we don't even tempt translators to touch them.  The above example would become `a b %s`, with `%s` dynamically replaced by `c`.  The ReST source could look something like `a b :notrans:`c``.

While I would indeed prefer a site-wide configuration like Takayuki proposed, it faces the same problem I outlined above — you just don't know which parts of translatable strings are not meant to be translated by looking from the outside.  Maybe, in addition to a new role _notrans_, `conf.py` could configure which roles also get the same treatment?
