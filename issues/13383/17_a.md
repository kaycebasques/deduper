This highlights a pitfall: The "`link text < ... >`" pattern has a small but very important difference when used in a Sphinx *:ref: role* vs. standard *rST hyperlink*:

In
~~~
:ref:`described above <submit_improvements>`.
~~~
the content in `<>` is always a *reference name* and does not use a trailing underscore as markup.

In a rST hyperlink, the content in `<>` can be a *URI reference* or an *alias hyperlink* depending on the presence/absence of a trailing underscore.
In a local documentation, 
~~~
`described above <submit_improvements>`__
~~~
points to the local file `submit_improvements` (which may or may not exist).

IMO, the use of angle brackets in the :ref: is unfortunate. 
While explicable because of the missing generic syntax for role parameters in reStructuredtext, it adds to the already considerable confusion around the rST hyperlink syntax.
