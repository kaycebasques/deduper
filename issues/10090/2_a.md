Unfortunately, in python3.9 or below, a NewType object does not have a correct module name where it's defined.
https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/issues/9560#issuecomment-913109874

To avoid the situation, Sphinx only refers its name via `__name__` attribute.
https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/blob/3ed92651d5c14343a127fcbcdcf5e44fcc6fc214/sphinx/util/typing.py#L135-L140

So please upgrade your interpreter to 3.10.

As a workaround, you might be able to deceive autodoc extension by setting `__name__` attribute. But it's not recommended way.

FYI: `__name__` and `__qualname__` should not contain module name. So your hack makes the NewType invalid.
