When an index directive interposes between a label and a section header, uncaptioned references to that label are not consistently resolved.

That is, the issue when it occurs is repeatable, but similar markup elsewhere may or not exhibit the same behavior.  Sometimes a specific issue can even be made to disappear simply by giving the label a different name!

We understand that Sphinx has a requirement to place labels immediately before section headers.  This doesn't seem entirely reasonable, given that a section might be tagged with multiple labels and multiple indexes.  In any case, it's the inconsistency that strikes us as most puzzling and most worthy of a bug report.

Attached is a simple example where it does occur reproducibly.
