'numfig' is rendering label with chapter number as "Fig. 5.1" for chapter 5. In my situation for writing a book, it has some "Appendix" chapters and I want to render numfig label as "Fig. A.1" in the chapter "Appendix A".

For example, declaring `.. numfig-prefix:: A` would be fine to set a prefix of label.
