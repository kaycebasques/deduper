I encountered this in multiple themes.  For a more systematic approach I looked at the themes provided by sphinx-themes.org.
Sadly, the .. content:: directive does not render anything (bug pending).  However, I found a list with "simple" in the admonitions and compared those to the list generated by .. toctree::

In total there were 113 themes, 41 showed a different style for the lists (probably because of the `<p>` but I have not verified that).  At least some (possibly all) of these themes do *not* include basic.css.
About 23 themes showed different spacing for the second level.  I classified those as "possibly flawed" but have not investigated the source of the problem.

So about 40% of themes might be affected.
