thanks @tk0miya -- I think a specialised `TOC` might be a nice way to go. I typically think of `frontmatter`, `backmatter` etc. from a [LaTeX / Book perspective](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/LaTeX/Document_Structure#The_document_environment) which usually defines each as:

1. Frontmatter
    - Half-title
    - Empty
    - Title page
    - Information (copyright notice, ISBN, etc.)
    - Dedication if any, else empty
    - Table of contents
    - List of figures (can be in the backmatter too)
    - Preface chapter
1. Mainmatter
    - Main chapters
1. Appendix
    - Some subordinate chapters
1. Backmatter
    - Bibliography
    - Glossary / Index

As a general rule `backmatter` usually behaves like `frontmatter` in terms of `style` (i.e. no numbering for chapters etc.) so it may not be necessary. I also know `Sphinx` has `Bibliography` and `Index` support already as directives.
