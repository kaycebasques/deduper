OK, so the LaTeX writer "sees" a `<paragraph>` with "ids".
The sample can be simplified to
~~~
.. _my-label:

A "named/labelled" paragraph.
~~~
The pseudoXML after parsing and applying transforms is

    <target refid="my-label">
    <paragraph ids="my-label" names="my-label">
        A “named/labelled” paragraph
    <paragraph>
        A reference to 
        <reference name="my-label" refid="my-label">
            my-label
        .

The Docutils LaTeX writer generates
~~~
\phantomsection\label{my-label}
A “named/labelled” paragraph

A reference to \hyperref[my-label]{my-label}.
~~~
(in both, Docutils 0.21.x and 0.22.rc2).

Adding "ids"-handling for `visit_paragraph()` will solve this test case in Sphinx.
