Sphinx LaTeX is now quite sophisticated at various levels:
- Python: `writers/latex.py` has `2491` lines and `writers/builders/latex/*` cumulate `1759` lines, the templates cumulate `311` lines
- LaTeX: the `.sty` and `.cls` files accumulate about `6900` lines of LaTeX code.

There are also `1181` lines of lisp code (very repetitive) for `xindy`, and the provided support for `latexmk` (also for Japanese context) and `make`.

I am throwing all these figures only to stress that it may be difficult to follow suit if the layer below were to experience drastic evolutions.
