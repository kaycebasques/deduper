The first `<target>` is indirect and should be ignored by the writer as it is already "resolved" (i.e. its `ids` and `names` attributes were moved to the next node by a transform).
The second `<target>`  has two IDs. These should become "labels" in LaTeX, because there is no `refid` or `refuri` attribute. This means references with `refid="somelabel"` as well as refernces with `refid="module-testmodule"` should point to the place of this target.

Yes.
The reason is, that in rare cases the `references.PropagateTargets` transform fails to 
transfer the `ids` from the target to the next element — the `<target>` element keeps its `ids` attribute and does not gain a `refid` attribute. 
In HTML, these empty targets are converted to empty anchors: `<div id="label-1a"></div>`. In LaTeX, they should generate a `\label{label-1a}`.
