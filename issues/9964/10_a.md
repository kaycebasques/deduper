For a brief addition to the pdflatex vs xelatex. As I briefly said pdflatex has no issues with utf-8 encoded characters with diacritics from European languages, they are supported out of the box. BUT, one must think of adding `latex_use_xindy = True` to preamble, because the legacy pdflatex indexing binary `makeindex` supports only ascii. Perhaps at next major release (5.0) Sphinx will make this configuration variable default to `True`. 

Usage of `xindy` is automatically the case with Sphinx if `latex_engine` is `'xelatex'`.
