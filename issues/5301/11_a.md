Note that unless you specify a [web safe font](https://www.w3schools.com/cssref/css_websafe_fonts.asp) or a [web font](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn/CSS/Styling_text/Web_fonts), the actual font that is used in a Sphinx document will depend on the what fonts are installed on a person's computer and their browser settings. The `font-family` CSS field usually specifies several fonts for fallback purposes.
