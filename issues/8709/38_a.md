* In  rST markup, we use "refeferene names" to specify anchors in the document and for references to them.
* In the "Document Tree" and its "native Docutils XML" representation, nodes can be identified by a "refname" in the *names* attribute or by an "identifier" in the *ids* attribute. Both are comparable to the "id" attribute of XML and HTML elements. Both, "refname" and "identifier" must be unique in their namespace. However, "DocTree" elements may have more than one "refname" and more than one "identifier".

In the Python implementation of the *DocTree* in the `docutils.nodes` module, there is a provision to access a document's elements by "identifier" (one of its *ids*) or "refname" (one of its *names*) via "internal attributes" of the "document" root element. This is documented in the `docutils.nodes` docstrings.
