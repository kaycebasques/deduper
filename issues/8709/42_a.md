A HTML element's "id" and "href" attributes written as part of the start tag by `docutils.writers._html_base.HTMLTranslator.starttag()`.

However, this "anchor *writer*" part does not do the work of maintaining global mappings. 
In Docutils, the mappings are *internal attributes* of the "document" element (`docutils.nodes.document`). They are filled by the parser component and updated by several "[transforms](https://docutils.sourceforge.io/docs/api/transforms.html)" (using auxiliary functions and methods in `docutils.nodes`).
Sphinx adds its own layer for the coordination of references across documents of a project.

For patching the section title "self-link" in Sphinx, look at the implementation added by Sphinx. (It differs from the implementation in `docutils.writers.html5_polyglot.HTMLTranslator.section_title_tags()`.)
