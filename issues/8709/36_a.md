*Reference names* are specified by the author in rST input to identify an element of the document as possible target. They are also used in rST [cross references](https://docutils.sourceforge.io/docs/ref/rst/restructuredtext.html#hyperlink-references) to internal targets.

*Identifiers* are used to identify fragments of the output document and in cross-links to them from other parts of the output document, Sphinx-generated project documentation or external documents somewhere in the internet. They must adhere to all limitations imposed by the  output-format.

Docutils recently completed the "[Docutils Document Tree](https://docutils.sourceforge.io/docs/ref/doctree.html)" reference. There you find a glossary with explanations for [reference names](https://docutils.sourceforge.io/docs/ref/doctree.html#reference-names) and [identifiers](https://docutils.sourceforge.io/docs/ref/doctree.html#identifiers). It includes links to detailled descriptions of used terms.
