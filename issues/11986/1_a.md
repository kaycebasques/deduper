### Describe the bug

[There is a class called `FrozenSpaceMeta`](https://github.com/janluke/cloup/blob/3899fbe66a5b339ea0bc78d181cbcea343b1bba5/cloup/_util.py#L122-L138) from the [`cloup`](https://github.com/janluke/cloup) project that I try to render with Sphinx's autodoc, but it always fails because its `__setattr__` method is raising an exception.
