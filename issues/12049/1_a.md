### Describe the bug

A theme that customizes `layout.html` cannot inherit from a theme that has an inherited `layout.html` and does not *explicitly* have its own, e.g., [default](https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/tree/master/sphinx/themes/default), [nature](https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/tree/master/sphinx/themes/nature), [sphinxdoc](https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/tree/master/sphinx/themes/sphinxdoc), or [traditional](https://github.com/sphinx-doc/sphinx/tree/master/sphinx/themes/traditional).
